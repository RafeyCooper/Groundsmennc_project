Mount Pleasant Landscaping Services We Offer

Lawn Maintenance

From weekly mowing to detailed edging, we keep your grass neat, trimmed, and healthy all season long.

Mulching & Seasonal Cleanups

Fresh mulch does more than look good. It helps your plants retain moisture and keeps weeds down. We also offer spring and fall cleanups to remove leaves, debris, and clutter so your yard is always ready for the next season.

Tree & Shrub Trimming

Overgrown bushes and low-hanging branches don’t just look messy. They can also block sunlight and cause plant health issues. We’ll prune everything the right way to keep your greenery looking sharp and growing strong.

Sod Installation & Aeration

Got bare spots or a struggling lawn? We can lay fresh sod or aerate your yard to improve soil health and help grass grow thicker.

Flower Beds & Garden Design

A splash of color can transform any space. We’ll help you plan and plant flower beds that add beauty without requiring tons of upkeep.

Hardscaping & Design
